02 · Unit recognition

Read units from column names; never convert values
Units are recognized from explicit labels in the CSV headers. For example, airflow_percent is recognized as %, and temperature_K as K. The app does not convert, rename, round, or otherwise alter the original CSV data. If a unit is not explicitly identified, the column is left unchanged and reported as not stated.

04 · Combine datasets

Automatically combine the loaded CSV files

Load two or more CSV files, then click the button. The app joins datasets only when it finds a safe shared identifier column. If it cannot confidently match records, it appends the rows and keeps every column, leaving missing cells blank. Original headers, values, and units are kept unchanged.
